# Lens: creative direction (Section B and CD-GDD-ALIGN)

Read at Section B when its specialist step runs, and at Step 5a-bis. It is
what the `creative-director` agent brought to both.

## Section B: Player Fantasy

- Give **2-3 candidate framings**, each anchored to **one specific player
  moment** (what the player is doing and sees when they feel it) and to a
  **quoted pillar** from the concept (from a one-page brief, its pitch and
  "what they feel" line).
- Match the game's established tone and words; a grimdark game's fantasy is
  not written like a cozy one's.
- Say for each framing what it asks of the rest of the design (a fantasy of
  mastery needs a skill ceiling; one of power needs visible growth).
- **A fantasy that works against a pillar is a conflict, not a style choice**:
  name the pillar and the clash and offer options. Never drop or soften the
  pillar silently.
- The person picks; you do not.

## CD-GDD-ALIGN (Step 5a-bis)

Read the GDD **as written on disk**, by section; not from memory of your
drafts. You drafted it, so look hardest for what is missing or quietly bent.

- Does every section serve the stated pillars? Name a rule, number or edge case
  that contradicts or weakens one, with the section it is in.
- Does Player Fantasy match the game's core fantasy, and do the rules deliver
  the moment it promises?
- Is a pillar served only by the fantasy text and by no rule?
- Verdict: **APPROVE**, **CONCERNS** [the sections and issues] or **REJECT**
  [pillar violations to redesign]. Without pillars, a brief's pitch line or a
  Player Fantasy to check against, the verdict is **NOT ASSESSED** with the
  missing input named; never APPROVE by default.

## Limits

No formulas or numbers, no code, no rewriting sections inside the gate: a
finding sends that section back through its own cycle.
