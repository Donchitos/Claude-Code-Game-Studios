# Chipset: idle-incremental

**Camera:** `static`; nothing moves the view, and the screen fills up with what you bought.

## Card defaults

- **rising-costs**: Each helper costs more every time you buy one, every button shows its price and
  what it adds, and the total and the rate per second sit at the top. Why: the next buy is always a
  choice between cheap now and better later. Break it when: prices are fixed on purpose, a cozy shop
  with no growth. [pecorella-1]
- **prestige-reset**: When growth slows, a reset trades the run for a lasting bonus that multiplies
  all output, and the button shows what you would gain now. Why: a wall becomes a choice, and each
  climb is faster than the last. Break it when: the game is one long climb the person asked for,
  with no reset. [pecorella-2]
- **away-earnings**: The game keeps earning while it is closed, from the time saved at the last
  visit, and coming back shows how long you were away and what you earned. Why: the game grew
  without you, so returning is a reward. Break it when: the game is about being there, and nothing
  should happen while you are gone. [clicker-heroes-1], [pecorella-2]

## Feel

- **first-click**: The first clicks pay at once, with a number popping from the button, and the
  first helper costs a handful of clicks and then earns on its own. Why: the game turns from working
  to watching in the first minute. Break it when: clicking is the whole game, a tapping race.
  [dark-room-1]
- **buy-shows**: A buy lands with a click, the helper appears on screen, and the rate jumps where
  you can see it. Why: each purchase answers at once and changes something you can see. Break it
  when: the game is only numbers on purpose, a spreadsheet of growth.
- **numbers-climb**: Totals count up smoothly, never in jumps, big numbers keep short names,
  thousand, million, billion, a setting switches to letters or scientific notation, and the digits
  do not jitter. Why: the player watches growth instead of reading strings of digits. Break it when:
  numbers stay small and exact, a game of a few precise counts. Hypothesis only.
- **milestone-burst**: A bar fills toward the next milestone of one helper, and crossing it
  multiplies that helper's output with a burst and a chime. Why: there is always a small win just
  ahead, and reaching it feels earned. Break it when: there is one kind of helper and nothing to
  collect. [pecorella-2]

## Feel variants

- **clicky**: Active and clicky: clicks stay worth it all game, with short bonuses to catch.
- **idle**: Calm and idle: it plays itself; you check in, buy and plan the next reset.
- **vast**: Tiny to vast: each stage changes what you make, a stall, then a city, then a planet.

## Choices that shape the pitch

- **What grows:** a business of helpers earning coins (default) · a chain of machines where one
  feeds the next · a story told through the numbers, each stage a new kind of thing.
- **Clicking:** matters early, then helpers take over (default) · matters all game · none, only
  buying.
- **Reset:** a prestige reset for a lasting multiplier (default; prestige) ·
  no reset, one long climb · resets stacked on resets (later).
- **Feeling:** the player feels growing power by watching a number that once crawled now race,
  because each buy multiplies the last and every reset climbs faster.

## Later, never in a one-shot

- Resets stacked on resets → add it as a feature
- Achievements and their bonuses → change it after play
- Research and upgrades that change the rules → grow it
- Diminishing returns on stats that could take over → add it as a feature
- Timed events → change it after play
- Paid boosts and ads → not built yet
- Saves that follow the player across devices → needs a server: not built yet
- Balance over hours and days of play → grow it

## In a mashup

Incremental leads the numbers, the costs and the reset. A management half leads the floor and what
customers do, and the numbers set the prices. An RPG half leads a hero whose upgrades the numbers
buy, and its fights must change as the numbers grow. A roguelite leads the run, and prestige is what
carries over. A story half leads the stages the numbers unlock.
