# Chipset: parkour

**Camera:** `first`; `orbit` for a runner or climber seen from behind.

## Card defaults

- **course-and-clock**: A route from a start to a finish you can see from the start, with a run
  timer on screen and your best time on the finish screen. Why: a clear line and a time to beat make
  every attempt count. Break it when: the city is free to roam, with no route and no clock.
- **runner-vision**: Everything you can climb, vault or run along shares one bright colour, and the
  rest of the world stays plain. Why: the route is read at speed, without stopping to look. Break it
  when: finding the way is the puzzle, a climb where each hold is searched for.
- **quick-restart**: A fall puts you back at the last checkpoint at once, with the clock still
  running. Why: a missed jump costs seconds, so the hard line gets tried again. Break it when: each
  run is one attempt, a single try at a dangerous climb.

## Feel

- **keep-momentum**: Speed builds while you run clean; a vault, a wall run, a slide or a roll
  pressed on a hard landing keeps it, and running into a wall loses it. Why: a clean line is felt as
  rising speed. Break it when: the game is a slow, careful climb where speed never matters.
- **forgiving-reach**: A ledge just out of reach is still caught, a jump pressed a moment after the
  edge still counts, and a gap almost wide enough is slipped through. Why: a near miss becomes a
  save, and the run keeps flowing. Break it when: exact reach is the challenge, a climb where every
  hold is measured.
- **speed-shows**: The view widens and the wind rises as you go faster, and reduced motion turns it
  down. Why: speed is felt without the controls changing. Break it when: the camera is far behind,
  where the world moving past shows the speed.
- **body-in-view**: In first person your hands reach out on vaults and climbs, and the view dips a
  little on a landing, with a setting to calm it. Why: the player feels a body, not a floating
  camera. Break it when: the camera is behind the runner, where the whole body shows.

## Feel variants

- **flowing**: Flowing: speed builds and carries, wall runs chain into slides, one long line.
- **precise**: Tight and precise: short exact jumps, quick stops, a time trial of clean inputs.
- **climber**: Slow and grounded: a climb where grip runs out and each hold is chosen with care.

## Choices that shape the pitch

- **What you do:** a run from start to finish against the clock (default) · a climb to the top with
  grip that runs out · deliveries across the rooftops, racing the weather.
- **Stakes:** a quick restart at checkpoints (default) · a chase, with a pursuer close behind ·
  none, roam and explore.
- **What the player sees:** through the runner's eyes, `first` (default) · from behind, `orbit`.
- **Feeling:** the player feels kinetic flow by chaining a vault into a wall run into a slide
  without losing speed, because each clean move carries its speed into the next.

## Later, never in a one-shot

- A ghost of your best run to race → change it after play
- A grapple and swinging → add it as a feature
- A city in layers from the sewers to the roofs → grow it
- Fights and guns between the moves, a movement shooter → add it as a feature
- Rain that makes holds slippery → add it as a feature
- Ropes and anchors on a climb → add it as a feature
- Advanced tricks kept as techniques → add it as a feature
- Times shared online, with a ranking → needs a server: not built yet

## In a mashup

Parkour leads movement, the camera and the route. A shooter half leads weapons and aim, and movement
keeps its momentum through the fight. A stealth half leads guards and sight, and parkour gives it
rooftop routes. A racing half leads rivals and the race, and the course stays a line to run. A
survival or delivery half leads what is carried and the weather.
