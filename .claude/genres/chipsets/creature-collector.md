# Chipset: creature-collector

Our research has no creature-collector row: catching, the party and the type loop are the genre's
staples, and their lines carry the RPG and turn-based entries that fit.

**Camera:** `topdown` for the world, `static` for battles.

## Card defaults

- **pick-a-starter**: You choose one of three starter creatures before the first step. Why: the
  first choice makes the team yours. Break it when: the first creature is part of the story, or
  caught in the opening scene.
- **weaken-to-catch**: A wild creature is caught by weakening it and then throwing a capture ball,
  and a weaker creature is easier to catch. Why: the catch is earned in the fight, at a risk the
  player chooses. Break it when: creatures join by being befriended, fed or helped, with no fight.
- **soft-loss**: Losing a battle sends you back to the last healing spot with the whole team healed
  and nothing lost. Why: the player keeps exploring instead of grinding back. Break it when: the
  person asks for hard stakes, a fainted creature gone for good.

## Feel

- **type-loop**: Every creature and move has a type, a few types beat each other in a loop, and the
  battle says when a hit is strong or weak. Why: a battle is something to read, not only a race of
  numbers. Break it when: nothing fights, a racing or care game where creatures differ in other
  ways.
- **quick-battles**: Four moves on fixed keys, the enemy's turn played fast, and battles that start
  and end in seconds. Why: the hundredth battle is as quick as the first. Break it when: battles are
  real time, steered by hand.
- **catch-moment**: The capture ball wobbles before it holds or breaks free, and a catch ends on a
  jingle and the creature's name. Why: the catch is the genre's high point, so it gets a beat of
  suspense and a reward. Break it when: creatures join with no catch, befriended or bought.

## Feel variants

- **snappy**: Snappy: quick menus, fast animations, the next battle seconds away.
- **cozy**: Cozy: a slower pace, creatures that react to you between battles, nothing rushed.

## Choices that shape the pitch

- **Battles:** turn-based, one creature each, moves from a menu (default) · real-time battles you
  steer by hand · a team of three that fights on its own from rules you set.
- **Stakes:** soft, back to the healer with nothing lost (default) · lose some money · a fainted
  creature is gone for good, a self-imposed hard mode.
- **Progression:** creatures level up and learn moves (default) · creatures change form at a
  level · the team grows only by catching.
- **What the player sees:** a top-down world and a separate battle screen (default) · one 3D
  world where battles happen in place, `orbit`.
- **Goal:** beat the first trainer at the end of the route (default) · catch every creature in
  one area · help the creatures, with no battles (cozy).
- **Feeling:** the player feels attached to their team by winning with a creature they caught
  themselves, because they weakened it and caught it in a fight they chose.

## Later, never in a one-shot

- Evolution trees → grow it
- Breeding → add it as a feature
- Trading and battles with other players → needs a server: not built yet
- A map of towns and a ladder of trainers → grow it
- Shops and items → change it after play
- A story → grow it
- Rare variants and protection from long unlucky streaks → change it after play
- Raising and caring for creatures between battles → add it as a feature

## In a mashup

The collection leads catching and the party. Turn-based battle leads combat rules and shows the
enemy's next move where the pitch wants planning. An adventure or RPG half leads the world and
progression. A racing half leads the race and the collection gives the racers. A farming or cozy
half leads the daily rhythm and creatures help with the work.
